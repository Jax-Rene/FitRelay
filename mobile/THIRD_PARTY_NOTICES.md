# Third-party exercise data

## free-exercise-db

- Source: https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db
- Imported revision: `b0eed061e1c832b3ed815fbaa4b45b3cdc14df49`
- Upstream dataset: 873 exercises with structured muscle, equipment, difficulty, instructions, and two-stage images.
- License: The Unlicense / public domain dedication. See the upstream `LICENSE.md`.
- Files used in this app: all 1,746 JPG files under `assets/exercise_library/`.
- Imported entries: all 873 upstream exercises.

The Chinese names, concise instructions, coaching cues, and defaults for 12 curated chest exercises are maintained by the Suilian AI project. The other 861 exercises retain their original English names and instructions. Application UI labels and the source-to-filter mappings are maintained by the Suilian AI project. Exercise instructions are general fitness references and are not medical advice.
