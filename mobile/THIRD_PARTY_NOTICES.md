# Third-party exercise data

## free-exercise-db

- Source: https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db
- Upstream dataset: 873 exercises with structured muscle, equipment, difficulty, instructions, and two-stage images.
- License: The Unlicense / public domain dedication. See the upstream `LICENSE.md`.
- Files used in this app: the 24 JPG files under `assets/exercise_library/`.
- Imported entries: `Leverage_Chest_Press`, `Butterfly`, `Leverage_Incline_Chest_Press`, `Dumbbell_Bench_Press`, `Incline_Dumbbell_Press`, `Dumbbell_Flyes`, `Barbell_Bench_Press_-_Medium_Grip`, `Barbell_Incline_Bench_Press_-_Medium_Grip`, `Decline_Barbell_Bench_Press`, `Pushups`, `Incline_Push-Up`, and `Push-Ups_With_Feet_Elevated`.

The Chinese names, concise instructions, coaching cues, defaults, and application UI are maintained by the Suilian AI project. Exercise instructions are general fitness references and are not medical advice.
